💡 Listing Tips from Phenice
What makes Richmond buyers click? Lead with the lifestyle, not the specs. Richmond buyers are community-driven — mention the neighborhood, the vibe, the walkability. Then back it up with the features.

Price it right the first time. Homes priced within 2% of market value sell 3x faster in the Richmond metro. Overpriced listings sit — and sitting creates doubt.

Photos + words = offers. Professional photography paired with a well-written description increases showing requests by up to 47%. Both matter.
